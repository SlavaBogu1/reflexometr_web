Medical facts

Factors known to affect reflexes and reaction time, and which reflexes can (and can't) be trained.

What degrades reflexes

Reflexometr does not diagnose any of the following — it simply measures reaction time, a metric known to move with each of them.

Age

Choice reaction time slows measurably per year of age past young adulthood.

Chronic stress

Sustained stress is associated with impaired cognitive-motor performance.

Alcohol

Even moderate blood-alcohol levels measurably slow reaction time and braking response.

Sleep deprivation

Extended wakefulness degrades reaction time comparably to legally-impairing alcohol levels.

Mental health conditions

Psychomotor slowing is a recognized, measurable feature of some mental health conditions.

Remote monitoring for aging adults

Reaction-time trend and variability, tracked over months, can help family members and caregivers notice emerging stress or cognitive change in an aging relative — non-invasively, and from any device with a browser.

Reflexometr is not a certified medical device and does not provide a certified medical or regulatory fitness-for-duty assessment. It is a reaction-time measurement and tracking tool; consult a qualified professional for any medical or fitness-for-duty decision.
