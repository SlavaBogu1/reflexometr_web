The research behind Reflexometr

Why reaction time matters, what's known to affect it, and where our test methods and paths come from — for visitors who want the evidence, not just the test.

Why reaction time matters

Reaction time is a simple, objective, repeatable measurement that reflects how quickly the brain perceives, decides, and acts. It changes with fatigue, stress, substances, age, and underlying health conditions — often before those factors are otherwise obvious.

Safety-critical professions

In roles where a fraction of a second matters, degraded reflexes are an operational safety risk, not just a personal inconvenience.

Pilots & aircrew

Fatigue is a persistent aviation safety concern; reaction-time-based vigilance tests are already used to study fitness for duty. [source]

Professional drivers

Slowed braking reaction time is directly implicated in drowsy- and impaired-driving crash risk. [source]

Drone / UAS operators

Remote-crew fatigue and stress affect the same perception-decision-action loop as in-cockpit flying. [source]

Military personnel

Split-second reactions under load are a core operational requirement in many military roles. [source]

Emergency services

First responders make rapid, high-stakes decisions where reaction speed and accuracy both matter. [source]

Professional athletes

Reaction-time baselines are already used in sports medicine, including concussion-recovery monitoring. [source]

What degrades reflexes

Reflexometr does not diagnose any of the following — it simply measures reaction time, a metric known to move with each of them.

Age

Choice reaction time slows measurably per year of age past young adulthood. [source]

Chronic stress

Sustained stress is associated with impaired cognitive-motor performance. [source]

Alcohol

Even moderate blood-alcohol levels measurably slow reaction time and braking response. [source]

Sleep deprivation

Extended wakefulness degrades reaction time comparably to legally-impairing alcohol levels. [source]

Mental health conditions

Psychomotor slowing is a recognized, measurable feature of some mental health conditions. [source]

Remote monitoring for aging adults

Reaction-time trend and variability, tracked over months, can help family members and caregivers notice emerging stress or cognitive change in an aging relative — non-invasively, and from any device with a browser. [source]

A monitoring platform, not a one-off quiz

Reflexometr is built for repeated, longitudinal use: staff wellness/evaluation programs, pre-offer candidate screening support, and ongoing personal or family tracking — the value comes from the trend, not a single score.

Ecosystem

The web platform is the core today; companion mobile apps and lab-grade measurement hardware are part of the longer-term product direction.

Reflexometr is not a certified medical device and does not provide a certified medical or regulatory fitness-for-duty assessment. It is a reaction-time measurement and tracking tool; consult a qualified professional for any medical or fitness-for-duty decision. The Gamer/Esports and Self State-Check paths in particular are self-awareness tools, not validated performance or impairment measurements.
