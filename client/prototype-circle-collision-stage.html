What's new vs. every existing test:

  • New stage shape (.collision-stage) reusing the same box/border/radius treatment as .stimulus-stage/.two-hand-stage, but with two independently-positioned circles instead of one centered orb.
  • Circles animate toward each other via requestAnimationFrame + performance.now() (never setTimeout) — starting at the stage's left/right edges, closing over the trial's resolved duration (1-5s).
  • Simple variant: both circles always 168px (matching the existing .orb size), constant speed for the whole trial.
  • Complex variant: each circle's size independently resolved per trial (±20% of the 168px baseline), and closing speed changes within a single trial's motion (not just trial to trial).
  • No color-change stimulus like the other tests (no green→red) — the "stimulus" here is the predicted collision moment itself; circles stay the accent color throughout.
  • Same trial-progress counter placement (top-center) as every other test's stage.
